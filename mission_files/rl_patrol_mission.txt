Inspection patrol with a single TurtleBot3 Burger in the Isaac Sim room. The TurtleBot3 starts at base coordinates (0.0, 0.0) facing the +x direction; all coordinates are in metres. There are boxes on the floor between the checkpoints, so the robot must avoid obstacles while driving. First, drive to checkpoint A at (1.5, 0.75) and scan the area with the LiDAR. Second, drive to checkpoint B at (1.5, -0.75) and scan the area with the LiDAR. Finally, rotate 90 degrees to face the room and report 'mission_complete' to the APM. Use exactly these numeric coordinates in move_to_point, do not use variables. Do not return to base. Only the TurtleBot3 takes part in this mission.
