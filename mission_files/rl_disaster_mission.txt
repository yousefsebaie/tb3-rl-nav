Earthquake search and rescue with a single TurtleBot3 Burger in a partially collapsed room in Isaac Sim. The TurtleBot3 starts at base coordinates (0.0, 0.0) facing the +x direction; all coordinates are in metres. Collapsed debris lies on the floor between the reported survivor locations, so the robot must avoid obstacles while driving. First, drive to the first reported survivor location at (1.5, 0.75), scan the area with the LiDAR, confirm the survivor with the camera using detect_with_camera('survivor'), and report 'survivor 1 found at (1.5, 0.75)' to the APM. Second, drive to the second reported survivor location at (1.5, -0.75), scan with the LiDAR, confirm the survivor with detect_with_camera('survivor'), and report 'survivor 2 found at (1.5, -0.75)' to the APM. Finally, rotate 90 degrees to face the room and report 'search complete, 2 survivors located' to the APM. Use exactly these numeric coordinates in move_to_point, do not use variables. Do not return to base. Only the TurtleBot3 takes part in this mission.
